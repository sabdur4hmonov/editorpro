## Umumiy pattern → standart

- Asosiy ibora katta, helper kichik; layout butun phrase uchun avvaldan saqlanadi. So‘z paydo bo‘lganda boshqa so‘z siljimaydi.
- Tez kirish + yumshoq settle. Tanlangan sample26 pill ~6–8f@25, sample33 prop ~9f@25, sample35 fold ~16f: bular universal davomiylik emas. New timeline soft enter 0.32s, exit 0.28s; brand soft/snappy/bouncy almashtiradi.
- Bir accent, yuz ochiq, dalilga mos rasm/UI; toza speaker oraliqlari. Interviewga kinetic creator zichligini majburlamaymiz.
- Grafik/footage transition bitta clock; mask/slide va overlapping exit. Oddiy fps oshirish smoothness bermaydi.
- Default soft; spring/blur urg‘uli joyga. Motion blur default off (CPU va o‘qilish); drift va zoom majburiy emas.

## Yetishmagan narsalar

Full Remotion timeline integration, output DOM geometry QA, segment cache, mixed-fps normalization without periodic duplicate frames, stable word layouts and bounded retry were not available together. Installed overlay scenes remain useful. Source easing, SFX identity and perceptual rhythm require human judgement; automatic QA is not a professional-design certificate.
