## Critical eye
- Strong: clear story arc with colour coding; result-first hook; the giant condensed keyword appears only 4-5 times (LEKIN, IELTS, INTERVYU, MAQSADLAR, VIZA OLISH) — rare, so it carries weight.
- Strong: transitions are short (2-5 frames): energy without slowing the story.
- Weak: CTA is only spoken + single words; no handle or card on screen at the end.
- Weak: handwriting captions (2.5-3 % height) are hard to read on bright B&W scenes.
- Excessive: 7 invert-wipes in 17 s (32-49 s) — the device repeats a lot.
- Note: the staged b-roll with an actress looks generated/staged (very clean, posed); our pipeline cannot produce it — only the user's own footage.
