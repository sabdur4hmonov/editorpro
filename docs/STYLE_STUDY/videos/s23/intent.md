## Critical eye
- Strong: every 1.3-3 s a new visual event; text always at chest height (46-62 % of frame); one idea per shot in the list part.
- Strong: CTA is concrete (comment a keyword) and visually separated (wide shot, platform logos).
- Weak: style switches a lot (italic grotesk, serif, neon script, caps, pills, 3D, script) — six type systems in 33 s; brand consistency is low.
- Excessive: 3D motion-blur words at 26-28 s are hard to read for ~12 frames; flicker exit at 1.6 s may read as an error.
- Weak: 7.7 s caption is left-aligned and small in the upper third — inconsistent with the rest.
